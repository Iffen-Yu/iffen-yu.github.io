**First author.** Accepted at NeurIPS 2026.

We study the limits of reusing a variational autoencoder (VAE) trained on another data source in latent diffusion models. We formulate VAE reuse and low-dimensional compression mathematically, derive upper bounds for score-learning error and structural lower bounds, and characterize the dimension dependence of training error when the VAE is retrained.
